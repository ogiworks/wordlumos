WordLumos Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

WordLumos is an English vocabulary app that runs entirely on your phone. There is no account, no sign-in, no advertising and no analytics.

We do not collect personal data. Your progress, test results, daily statistics, exam date and settings are stored only in a local database on your device and are never sent to any server. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Internet use. The app connects to the internet only to check for app updates. That request goes to Expo's update server (expo.dev) and contains the platform, the app's version information, the update channel and a random installation identifier created when the app is installed. The identifier is not linked to your name, any account or your device's hardware identifiers; it is used only to deliver the correct update. Expo's privacy policy: https://expo.dev/privacy

Backups are written to a file and handed to the system share sheet; you choose where the file goes. The app never uploads it by itself.

Feedback. The "Send feedback" button in Settings opens your own email app. The draft ends with the app version, update details and your phone's Android version and model; you see them before sending and can delete anything. The email reaches us only if you send it, and is used only to understand and answer your message.

Text-to-speech uses your phone's own TTS service. Notifications are scheduled and shown on the device.

Children. The app is intended for people preparing for university-level exams and is not directed at children under 13.
